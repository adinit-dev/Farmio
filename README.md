# 🌱 Farmio

**Farmio** is a community-driven platform where farmers can ask questions, share experiences, and get **real, practical solutions** from other farmers and agriculture enthusiasts.

The goal is simple:  
👉 **Direct conversations. Real answers. No noise.**

---

## 🚜 Why Farmio?

Farmers often struggle to find trustworthy, region-relevant advice. Farmio solves this by enabling:
- Open discussions
- Peer-to-peer problem solving
- Experience-based solutions instead of generic articles

No experts talking down. Just farmers helping farmers.

---

## 💬 Core Features

- 🗣️ **Discussion-based Q&A**  
  Farmers can post questions and respond with real-life solutions.

- 🌾 **Community Knowledge Sharing**  
  Crop issues, soil health, irrigation, fertilizers, weather impact, and more.

- 🤝 **Farmer-to-Farmer Interaction**  
  Learn directly from people who’ve faced the same problems.

- 📱 **Simple & Accessible UI**  
  Designed to be easy to use, even for first-time users.

---

## 🛠️ Tech Stack

- **HTML** – Structure and content  
- **CSS** – Styling and layout  
- **JavaScript** – Interactivity and logic  

No heavy frameworks. Fast, lightweight, and focused.
